What comes through the door

Bring us the thing you keep meaning to hang.

Four brothers-in-arms of the wall: art, objects, mirrors, and the pieces that need a little rescuing first. Every one gets cut, joined and fitted here on Route 46 — nothing ships out.

01 / Gilt

Fine art & prints

Watercolours, oils, editions, the print you carried home from Rome. Conservation mounting so it comes back out unmarked.

02 / Walnut

Shadow boxes

Jerseys, medals, christening gowns, the first pair of shoes. Depth built to the object, stitched or pinned so it sits still.

03 / Silver

Mirrors & glass

Cut, bevelled and framed to your wall, with the hanging hardware rated well past the weight. We'll tell you where the stud is.

04 / Ebony

Repair & re-fit

Yellowed mats, sagging tape, a corner that let go in the move. Often the frame is fine and only the inside needs replacing.

Start to wall · about one week

Four steps, and only one of them is yours.

No appointment, no design fee. Walk in with the piece — or a photo of the wall it's going on — and we'll work it out at the counter.

Step 01

Bring it in

Unwrapped is fine. If it's oversized, call first and we'll meet you at the door on the Route 46 side.

Walk-ins · Mon–Sat
Step 02

Choose the corner

We hold samples against the piece until one stops the conversation. Mat, moulding and glass, priced as we go.

Hundreds of mouldings
Step 03

Cut and joined here

Mats cut on the bevel, corners glued and pinned under clamp, everything hinged so it can come back out.

1½″ typical rabbet
Step 04

Pick it up

Wired, bumpered and wrapped, with the hanging hardware in the bag. We'll call the day it's ready.

Approx. 7–10 days

You'll be handed samples by whoever owns the place. That's the whole pitch.
